Overlay Studio

Make broadcast graphics on your Mac and put them on air from a simple button board. Your graphics show up in OBS, vMix or any tool with a browser source.

Get it free on the Mac App Store

How to use it

Build it. Type it. Take it.

  1. Make a template: build a lower third on a blank canvas, or import a layered Photoshop file.
  2. Open Play and add the template to your button board.
  3. Type the names. Spell check is built in.
  4. Press Take (or Space) to put it on air. Press Out to take it off.
  5. Add the Program link from Feed links to OBS or vMix as a browser source.

What you can build

Graphics that look on-air.

  • Text, shapes, shadows, outlines and glow.
  • PNG images with transparency.
  • Photoshop import, layer by layer.
  • In and Out transitions for each template.

Running a show

Preview and Program.

  • Preview and Program side by side.
  • 16:9 or 9:16 vertical.
  • One switch to share a feed outside your network.
