Bloom

Plant the seeds. Grow the picture. A quiet garden puzzle. No ads.

How to play

Every seed spreads its own way.

  1. Put your seeds on the plots.
  2. Red poppies spread in a +. Blue cornflowers spread in an ×. Yellow sunflowers jump two plots.
  3. Tap the watering can. Everything grows at once.
  4. Two colors on one plot make pebbles. Nothing grows there.
  5. Match the picture on the seed packet to clear the garden.

Every day

The daily garden.

  • One new garden every day, with a few tries.
  • Game Center boards and trophies, if you want them.

Good to know

Plays offline.

  • No ads. No accounts. No data collected.
  • Coins come from playing. The shop also sells coin packs, a Starter Bundle and an hour of endless hearts.
  • Progress is saved on the device.
