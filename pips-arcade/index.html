Kids games · iPhone

Pip's Arcade

Welcome to DotVille, Pip's little paper town. Every building is something to do. Two games, a Claw Machine, a Prize Counter and a room of your own. No ads.

Sky Patrol

Fly. Pop. Cheer them up.

  • Fly a paper plane and pop grumpy clouds until they cheer up.
  • 400 levels across Rainbow Skies, Cosmic Coast, Thunder Tops and Frosty Flight.
  • A boss at the end of every world.

Splat Defense

Paint the town back.

  • Baron Bleach and Count Socksworth want the town colorless.
  • Build paint towers along the path and splat the Blanks back into color.
  • 200 levels in Paint Park and Spin Cycle.

The Claw Machine

Line it up. Grab a Dot.

  1. Pip shows you how, then it's your turn.
  2. Line up the claw and grab.
  3. The first Dot you win each day goes to your room.
  4. Win the same Dot again and it grows: small, medium, large.
  5. More wins that day pay tickets.

Tickets and prizes

Play to earn. Never to buy.

  • Every mission pays tickets. More stars, more tickets. Beat your best and you get the difference.
  • Pip's Prize Counter trades tickets for Dots, hats for Pip, room decor and friends from DotVille Quest.
  • Tickets come from playing. They can never be bought.

My Room and Pip

Make it yours.

  • Everything you win shows up in My Room.
  • Drag anything where you like and it stays there.
  • Tap a Dot or a friend to read who they are.
  • Stars from every game open hats for Pip. A daily challenge adds bonus stars.

Made for kids

Safe by design.

  • No ads. No accounts. No data collected.
  • The first 10 levels of every land are free. More levels are one purchase per land, behind a grown-up check. Restore Purchases is on the Pick a Land screen.
  • Progress is saved on the device and in your own iCloud.
  • Game Center is optional. Only the best score goes to the leaderboard.
