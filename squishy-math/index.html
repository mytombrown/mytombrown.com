Squishy Math Trail

A board game your kid already knows how to play, with math built in. Roll, solve, hop. No ads, no purchases, nothing online.

Coming to the App Store

How to play

Roll. Solve. Hop.

  1. Pick a player. Name, age and a squishy. Each kid gets their own.
  2. Roll the die. Tap it on your turn.
  3. Solve the problem. It is sized to your age. Tap the right answer.
  4. Hop. Right answer, hop that many spaces. Wrong answer, stay put.
  5. Land on a star for bonus points.
  6. Reach the finish to win the level. The next trail is longer and the numbers are bigger.

Math that fits the child

Every player gets their own math.

  • Ages 4–5: adding only.
  • Ages 6–7: adding and subtracting.
  • Ages 8–9: adding, subtracting, multiplying.
  • Ages 10–12: all four, division included.
  • Numbers grow every level. A 5-year-old starts at sums to 10. A 12-year-old ends up at 1,000.
  • Two players, two ages. Take turns on one board. Each kid gets problems for their own age.

Levels and points

Longer trails, bigger numbers.

  • Level 1 is 12 spaces. Four more each level, up to 48.
  • 10 × level for every correct answer.
  • 50 × level for finishing the trail.
  • 25 points for a star.
  • Best score is remembered per player.

Squishies, coins and the shop

Play to unlock.

  • Twelve squishies: Kitty, Bunny, Bear, Dino, Froggy, Uni, Puppy, Panda, Axo, Owl, Piggy and Dragon. Nine colours each.
  • Every point becomes a coin. Coins are earned by playing, never bought.
  • Everyone starts with pink Kitty. Buy the rest in the shop with coins.
  • They cheer when you get it right and sulk when you don't.

Achievements

Nine badges to earn.

  • From First Hop to Diamond Star, Mountain Top and Math Whiz.
  • Each pays bonus coins the first time it is earned.
  • Each player's stats are kept: coins, best game, levels finished, games played, right-answer rate.

Made for families

Safe by design.

  • No ads. No purchases. No accounts.
  • Nothing online. The app has no internet connection at all. Players live on the phone.
  • Big red confirm panels before stopping a game or deleting a player. A stray tap loses nothing.
  • Sound with a one-tap mute. Respects the silent switch.
