Surface Remote for Companion

Your iPhone or iPad becomes a button surface for Bitfocus Companion. Press buttons, change pages, see live button text and colour. On your home screen it is called Surface Remote.

Version 1.1 (build 5) · in TestFlight · Free · Save Connection $1.99

Getting started

Five steps to a working surface.

  1. Install Companion. Get Bitfocus Companion from bitfocus.io and run it on a computer on the same Wi-Fi or network as your phone.
  2. Turn on Satellite. In Companion's Settings, make sure the Satellite WebSocket listener is on. Its port is 16623 unless you changed it.
  3. Allow Local Network. iOS asks once. Tap Allow.
  4. Tap your Companion. The Connect screen lists it under Found on network. Tap it. The app switches to the Surface screen when it is Online.
  5. Not listed? Type the computer's address in Host (Companion's launcher window shows it, for example 192.168.1.50) and the port in Port. Tap Connect.

The free app starts blank each time it opens. Save Connection ($1.99) remembers your last connection and connects to it when the app opens.

Save Connection · $1.99 in-app · new in 1.1

One Companion, remembered.

  • Free: the full surface, but host, port and surface settings are forgotten when the app closes.
  • Save Connection: host, port, surface name and surface settings are kept.
  • Opens connected. The last connection is used at launch. Turn off Auto-connect last in Settings to stop that.
  • Bought the paid app before 1.1? Save Connection is already yours. Nothing to buy.
  • Buy or restore it in the Settings tab (gear) › Purchases. Multi-Instance includes it.
  • Lock icons mark paid features. Tap one to see the price.

Multi-Instance · $4.99 in-app

Many Companions. Two at once on iPad.

  • Save Server keeps each one in the Servers list. Star one to connect to it first.
  • Switcher (⇄) on the Surface screen, next to the page arrows. Pick a server. The surface shows Connecting, then its buttons.
  • iPad two-up: tap the split button. Two surfaces at once. Each has its own ⇄ and page arrows.
  • Biggest keys, automatically. On iPad the surfaces sit top and bottom or side by side — whichever gives bigger keys. Rotate and it re-fits.
  • Companion lists each side as its own surface. Two sides on one Companion is fine.
  • Restore Purchases is in Settings › Purchases. One purchase covers your devices on the same Apple Account.

Pages and buttons

It follows Companion.

  • Tap a button to press it. Hold to hold it.
  • Up / Down buttons change the Companion page. The page number shows at the top.
  • Button text and colour update live from Companion.
  • Rotate button (top of the Surface screen) stands the buttons upright: hold the phone upright and Companion's columns become rows. Tap it again for sideways.
  • In Companion, the phone shows under Surfaces. Set its page and name there.

Settings tab · new in 1.1

Tap the gear.

  • The gear tab holds App, Connection, Surface, Purchases and Diagnostics. It turns with the phone.
  • Rows and Columns set the grid. Default 4 × 8.
  • Artwork Off shows text and colour only. Off is fastest. 72 or 96 shows Companion's button pictures.
  • Text Size makes labels bigger or smaller.
  • Advanced layout adds encoder (knob) controls.
  • Auto reconnect comes back by itself if Companion restarts.
  • Settings › App: Keep screen awake while connected stops the phone locking mid-show.
  • Settings › App: Theme: System, Light or Dark.
  • The Connect screen turns with the phone too: one column upright, two side by side sideways.

Dashboard · new in 1.1

Your keys, one screen.

  • Open the Dashboard tab. Tap Add keys.
  • Use the page arrows to find a key on any Companion page. Tap it to add it. Tap Done.
  • Keys stay live: text, colour and state follow Companion. Tap to press.
  • Edit removes keys. The Dashboard is kept for each server.
  • Needs Companion 5 with Satellite subscriptions on. If they are off, the tab says so.

Operator mode · new in 1.1

Hand a crew member only their keys.

  • On your Dashboard tap Share. Name the role, pick its keys, tap Show QR.
  • On the crew phone: Connect › Operator. Scan the QR. That phone shows only those keys.
  • Your phone can be off. The crew phone talks to Companion directly.
  • Revoke in the same Share sheet. Their keys go within seconds.
  • One-time setup: in Companion, add a custom variable named surfaceremote_roles. The app tells you if it is missing. Companion's HTTP API must be on.

Troubleshooting

If it will not connect.

  • Stuck on Connecting or Offline? Check iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. This app must be on. Without it the app cannot reach Companion.
  • Wrong port. The app uses the WebSocket port (16623), not the TCP port (16622).
  • Same network. Phone and Companion computer must be on the same network. Guest Wi-Fi often blocks devices from seeing each other.
  • Firewall. Allow Companion through the computer's firewall.
  • Companion closed or restarted. The status pill reads Offline. With Auto reconnect on, it comes back Online when Companion does.
  • Buttons slow to update? Set Artwork to Off.
  • Up / Down show no picture? With Artwork off they show as text labels. That is expected.
  • Want the phone upright? Tap the rotate button on the Surface screen.
  • Connection forgotten every time? That is the free app. Save Connection keeps it.
  • Saved servers gone? They are kept on the device. Buy or restore Multi-Instance to see them again.
  • A PIN pad covers the keys? Companion's PIN lock is on. Type the PIN on the app's keypad.
  • Need help from support? Settings › Diagnostics › Share Log sends the connection log. Attach it to your email.

Privacy

No account. Nothing collected.

The app talks only to the Companion server you use. Saved servers stay on your device. The camera is used only to scan an Operator QR code, on the device. Privacy policy.
