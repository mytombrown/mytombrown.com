Recap

Record anything. Read the transcript and notes. Then listen back as a short newscast or a two-voice podcast.

Coming soon

How it works

Record, stop, listen.

  1. Tap Record. Add typed notes, highlights or photos while it records.
  2. Tap Stop. Recap writes the transcript on your device.
  3. Read the notes. A summary and key points. Tap a point to hear that moment.
  4. Listen back. As a newscast with one voice, or an interview with two.
  5. Daily Recap. Pick a day and hear every meeting from it.
  6. Ask. Ask a question and Recap finds the meeting with the answer.

More

Works where you are.

  • Templates: General, 1:1, Sales call, Stand-up, Lecture, Interview.
  • Apple Watch: record on your wrist. It lands on your iPhone.
  • Mac: can record call audio too. The transcript says who is You and who is Them.
  • Siri and Spotlight: find a recording by name, or say "play the podcast".
  • iCloud Drive keeps your recordings on all your devices.
  • Plain files: audio, transcript and notes are normal files you can open.

Your data

On your device first.

  • Apple Intelligence on your device makes the notes by default. Needs a device that supports it.
  • Optional: connect Claude or ChatGPT with your own key.
  • Auto-delete after 30 days, or mark a recording Keep Forever.
