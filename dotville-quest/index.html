DotVille Quest

A turn-based adventure in a world made of paper, felt and paint. The Color Fountain has turned white. Find out who did it, with Pip.

How to play

Walk, find clues, splat the Blanks.

  1. Walk: drag anywhere for a thumb-stick, or tap a spot to walk there.
  2. Talk to everyone. Pin each clue to the Clue Board; each one opens the next part of the land.
  3. Battle: when your bar fills, pick Splat, a skill, an item, Guard or Run.
  4. Grow: earn experience, level up, learn skills. Spend buttons at the Juice Stand and the Paint Shop.
  5. Rest and save at the warp fountain on every map.

Good to know

Made for families.

  • No ads. No purchases. No accounts. No data collected.
  • Plays offline. Saves as you walk.
  • Chapter 1, Paint Park, is here. More lands are on the way.
