Rift Wardens

How to play

Build, start, survive.

  1. Pick a rift, then a board. Dust Plains board 1 teaches the game.
  2. Tap a square next to the path and pick a tower.
  3. Tap START. Enemies walk the path to your base.
  4. Between waves you get up to 2 minutes to build. The score clock stops. Tap SKIP to go now.
  5. Tap a tower to upgrade it. Three paths each.
  6. Earn XP every wave. Spend it on new towers and higher tower levels.

The rifts

Five worlds, five wardens.

  • Dust Plains: aliens. Warden Kade Marrow. 10 boards.
  • Grave Rift: zombies. Warden Ash Holloway. 20 boards. New tower: Flame Tower.
  • Fang Rift: monsters. Warden Bryn Thorne. 20 boards. New tower: Frost Cannon.
  • Iron Rift: soldiers. Warden Rook Danner. 20 boards. New tower: Railgun.
  • Hive Rift: giant bugs. Warden Tamsin Reed. 20 boards. New tower: Flak Tower.
  • Endless mixes every rift you've opened. Daily Challenge: one board a day, the same for everyone.

How to beat them

Commander Vale's field reports.

  • Skitters: small and fast. Machine Gun.
  • Shellbacks: bullets bounce off. Arc Coil cuts through armor.
  • Brutes: huge and slow. Rocket Launcher.
  • The Overlord: put Mortars on a bend so it walks through every blast.
  • Bloaters: burst into 4 Crawlers. Kill them early, far from your base.
  • Grave Lord: raises the fallen. Flame Tower: what burns stays down.
  • Hidebacks: heal. Freeze them with Frost Cannon. Frozen hides don't heal.
  • Leapers: jump ahead. Cover the far end of the path too.
  • Fang King: its roar speeds up the pack. Freeze it, then focus fire.
  • Shield Troopers: Mortar drops over the shield. Railgun goes through it.
  • Medics: heal soldiers near them. Take the Medic out first.
  • Iron Walker: Railguns lined up on a long straight.
  • Hornets: they fly. Mortars can't reach them. Flak Tower.
  • Burrowers: dig under the path. Build where they come back up.
  • Hive Queen: flies and calls Hornets. Flak first, then everything.
