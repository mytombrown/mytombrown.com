How to play

Six stops make a day.

  1. Pick a player. Same squishies, coins and shop as the other Squishy games.
  2. Go stop to stop. Bus, school, lunch, park, dinner, bed. The sun moves across the sky.
  3. Read the clock. Pick the right time from four.
  4. Or set the hands. Drag them, or use the + and − buttons.
  5. Wrong? You get a hint and a second try. Miss twice and the squishy misses the bus. Next stop.
  6. Finish the day. A full day moves you up a level.

The levels

Easy to hard.

  • O'clock, then half past, then quarter past and quarter to.
  • Five minutes, then to the minute.
  • Time that passes: "The bus comes in 20 minutes. What time is that?"
  • Digital to hands and hands to digital.
  • Rush days: a clock on every stop.
  • Older kids start higher. Pick the age and the game picks the level.
