Puzzle games · iPhone

One More

Quick puzzle games. One daily board for everyone. Race a rival on the same board. No ads, ever.

On the App Store

How to play

Pick a game, play a round.

  1. Pick a game. Fourteen quick games on the home screen.
  2. Tap ? any time. A How to Play card explains the game. The round pauses.
  3. Play the daily. One game a day. Same board for everyone. One try.
  4. Earn points. Every round pays points toward your rank.
  5. Go versus. Pick a game and a rival. You both play the same board. Higher score wins.
  6. Rematch. One tap when it's over.

The games

Fourteen to pick from.

  • Stackit, Way Out, Quick Tap, Doubles, Triples, Swap 3.
  • Five Letters, Tower, One Line, Pair Up, Pour, Mini Sudoku.
  • Tic-Tac-Toe: classic or Ultimate. Play the phone, a friend on the same phone, or online.
  • Crossword: type a theme and Apple Intelligence makes the puzzle on your iPhone. No Apple Intelligence? You can still solve one a friend sends.

Ranks and versus

Play people at your level.

  • Achievements in Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum.
  • Ranks from Just One to Legend. Each rank has its own weekly leaderboard.
  • Game Center leaderboards: overall, daily and per game.
  • Versus: a Game Center friend, someone you played before, anyone, or pass one phone back and forth.
  • Styles like tile colors and backgrounds are earned, never sold.

Free, or One More+

No ads on either.

  • Free: 5 plays an hour. The daily challenge and versus are always free.
  • One More+: unlimited plays. $1.99 a month or $9.99 a year.
  • No tracking. No account to make. Progress saves to your own iCloud.
