Cable Checker

A desktop widget that shows what every USB-C port on your Mac is doing.

Download for Mac

Version 1.0 · macOS 14 or later · free · signed and checked by Apple

What it shows

Each port, at a glance.

  • What is plugged in. A phone, a drive, a dock, by name.
  • How fast it is. 480 Mbps, 10 Gbps, 40 Gb/s.
  • How it is connected. USB 2, USB 3, Thunderbolt, DisplayPort.
  • Charging. Which port charges your Mac, and at how many watts.
  • The cable. Smart (e-marked), active or optical.

Install

Two minutes.

  1. Download and open the zip.
  2. Drag Cable Checker into Applications.
  3. Open it once. Click Open when your Mac asks. It runs quietly in the background.
  4. Add the widget. Right-click the desktop, choose Edit Widgets, find Cable Checker.

Good to know

What a Mac will not tell an app.

  • A cable's top rating (like 240 W or 40 Gb/s) stays inside the cable. Cable Checker shows what the cable is doing now.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac. No account, no network, no tracking.
