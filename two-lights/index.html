Two Lights

Two lamps. Two walls. Two pictures. Place blocks on the floor so both shadows match. No ads.

How to play

Build the shadow.

  1. Each lamp shines at the wall across from it.
  2. Tap a floor square to add a block. Blocks cast shadows on both walls.
  3. Make both shadows match the two pictures.
  4. You only get a few blocks. One tall stack can fill both walls at once.
  5. Tap Lights on to check.

Five worlds

100 rooms. A new rule in each world.

  • Classic — the rules above.
  • Stuck blocks — grey blocks can't move.
  • Holes — nothing stands on a dark square.
  • Glass — glass casts no shadow.
  • Third light — a lamp overhead: match the floor too.

Every night

Tonight's room.

  • One new room every day, the same for everyone.
  • Game Center boards and trophies, if you want them.

Good to know

Plays offline.

  • No ads. No accounts. No data collected.
  • Coins come from playing. The shop also sells coin packs, the Night Owl Kit and an hour of endless hearts.
  • Progress is saved on the phone.
