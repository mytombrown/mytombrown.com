Squishy Word Climb

See the clue, spell the word, climb the ladder. Then turn this week's spelling list into a test the phone reads out loud.

In TestFlight

How to play

Word, clue, clock, ladder.

  1. Pick a player. Same squishies, coins and shop as Squishy Math Trail.
  2. Read the clue. An emoji and the first letter. Words are picked for your age.
  3. Spell it. Tap the letters, or swipe through them and lift to submit. Undo takes the last one back.
  4. Beat the clock. Take too long and hints appear: more letters, better clues, fewer points.
  5. Climb. Each word is a rung. Five rungs and you finish the level.
  6. Stuck? Skip costs 5 points. The next level is harder.

Spelling Test mode

Your list, read aloud.

  • Make a list per kid. Type it, paste it, or take a picture of the sheet from school. The phone reads the words off the photo.
  • The phone says each word. Tap repeat to hear it slower. It plays even on silent.
  • Spell on the big ABC pad. Tap or swipe.
  • 10 points first try, 5 on the second. Three misses and the word comes back later in the same test.
  • Results card with stars, coins and the words to practise. Missed words come first next time.
  • Three badges: Test Taker, Perfect Test, Study Buddy.
