File Sweep

Find duplicate files on your Mac, drives and network shares. Then move or delete the extras.

How it works

Add a place, search, clean up.

  1. Add locations. Drives, folders or network shares. Drop them in or tap Add Location.
  2. Pick a search. Find Duplicates, Find File Type, or Search Name.
  3. Narrow it down. By type, size, or video resolution.
  4. Search. Progress bars show how far along it is.
  5. Select. Pick files, or all but the oldest or newest copy.
  6. Move or delete. Delete asks first. It cannot be undone.

Good to know

Built to be safe.

  • Duplicates are real copies. Files are matched by their contents, not their names.
  • It keeps one copy. It will not let you delete every copy in a set.
  • Types: images, videos, audio, documents, archives, or your own list.
  • Name search ignores upper and lower case.
  • Only your locations are saved. A share that is not connected is skipped.
