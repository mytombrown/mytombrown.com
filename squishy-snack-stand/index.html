How to play

Five customers make a shift.

  1. Pick a player. Same squishies, coins and shop as the other Squishy games.
  2. Serve the line. Each squishy customer is one round.
  3. Answer the money question. Name the coin, count it, or make change.
  4. Right? The customer smiles. You get points and a tip in the jar.
  5. Wrong? You get a hint and a second try.
  6. Finish the shift. A full shift moves you up a level.

The levels

Easy to hard.

  • Name the coin: penny, nickel, dime, quarter. Drawn at their real sizes.
  • Count one kind of coin, then mixed coins.
  • Did they pay enough? Yes or no.
  • Make change by tapping coins onto the tray.
  • Pay the supplier for new stock, with bills too. Then dollars and cents.
  • Rush hour: a clock for each customer and a line of five.

Made for families
