Sight Line: Color Pop

Place tiles. Line up three of a color. Watch them pop. A one-screen puzzle you can play in a minute or all evening. No ads.

How to play

Three in a line pop.

  1. Drag a tile from your tray onto the 6 by 6 board.
  2. Three of a color in one row or column pop, even with gaps between them.
  3. Every three tiles, a stray drops on the ringed square.
  4. Fill the board and the game is over. Beat your best.

Every day

Today's Board.

  • One board a day, the same tiles for every player.
  • One try. Keep your streak going.
  • Game Center boards for Best Score and Today's Board.

Three looks

Clean, Glossy or Glowing.

  • Each look has its own sounds.
  • Your best, streak and settings follow you with iCloud.

Good to know

Plays offline.

  • No ads. Ever. No accounts. No data collected.
  • Free to download, with a 3-day free trial. Sight Line Full is one purchase.
