Family Meal Planner

Plan the week's meals, build the shopping list in one tap, and share it all with the family.

In development

What it does

Meals, plan, groceries.

  • Meals. Your own library with ingredients, the recipe, how long it takes and a photo.
  • Scan a recipe. Point the camera at a recipe and it becomes a meal.
  • Plan. Pick a meal for each day and slot. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, whichever you turn on.
  • Suggest meals. On phones with Apple Intelligence, the app fills empty slots with variety, on the phone.
  • Groceries. One tap builds the week's list from the plan. Checkmarks sync to the family.
  • Family sharing. Invite the family over iCloud. Everyone sees the same plan and list.
